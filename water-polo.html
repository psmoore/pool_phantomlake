About

Join the exciting and fast-growing sport of water polo! Phantom Lake Water Polo is a summer league team sponsored by PLC under the umbrella of the Midlakes Water Polo League, a recreational league that emphasizes sportsmanship, education, skill development, and participation through friendly competition. PLC is one of 14 teams in Midlakes Water Polo.

Water polo is open to all members. There are no tryouts, and all are welcome regardless of experience or ability. Teams are coed and compete against like-aged opponents: 10 and under, 12 and under, 14 and under, and 15 and up.

  • Team fee: $128 per player
  • Who: PLC members, or members of another Midlakes pool without a water polo team
  • Required: Midlakes Hold Harmless Agreement, concussion form, U.S. Center for SafeSport training

The summer team's practice and game times will be entered on the pool calendar when the season is set.
