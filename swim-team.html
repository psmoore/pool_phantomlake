About Phantom Lake Swim Team

A recreational summer swim program focused on skill development, teamwork, and fun. Swimmers practice through the season and compete in age-group races against other Midlakes Swim League teams. All ability levels are welcome and there are no tryouts; swimmers should be able to swim across the width of the pool (about 42 feet).

  • Team fee: $255 per swimmer, plus the $16.80 annual Swimmingly fee
  • Mini Phantoms (age 4+, comfortable in the water but not yet swimming 25 yards): $100 per swimmer
