What's different in this preview

Today on phantomlakeclub.org

  • The Hours page is four Google Calendars layered in week mode: pool, lessons and programs, team, social.
  • The off-season shows nothing, though the pool is booked most weeknights by Northwest Water Polo Club.
  • Program pages type their dates and times by hand, separately from the calendars.

In this preview

  • One schedule for the pool, drawn lane by lane: members' swims, clinics and renters side by side.
  • Every hour on every page is read from that schedule, so a change shows up everywhere at once.
  • The renter keeps its own practices in the same calendar, so the club sees them without asking; see the Northwest Water Polo Club preview.
  • Members can follow a program from the calendar's filter row and get an email when it changes.
